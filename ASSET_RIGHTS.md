# Asset and screenshot rights

This is a public-facing case study. The screenshot depicts the creator's own branded app and contains personal photography and portrait artwork. The screenshot and visual assets are for showcasing the project only and are not licensed for third-party reuse.

Before publishing, check that everyone depicted has consented to public display and that any source photographs, celebrity references and third-party imagery are authorised for the intended usage. If unsure, replace the screenshot with a permission-cleared image.
